Design for the Mind.

Nice to meet you! This is Stella, a PhD student in Neurobiology and Behavior at Columbia University. Trained in cognitive science and computer science, I study how the brain learns from reward and makes decisions, using computational models to connect behavior, brains, and machines. Welcome to my scientific and creative space!
