About Me

I am currently pursuing my PhD in Human-Computer Interaction, focusing on user experience design and accessibility research. With extensive experience in UX/UI design and user research, I bridge the gap between design practice and academic research.

My work encompasses design systems, accessibility audits, voice interfaces, and healthcare technology design. I believe in creating experiences that are not only beautiful but also inclusive and accessible to all users.
