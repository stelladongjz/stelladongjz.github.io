Rewire the Mind.

Hi! This is Stella, a PhD student in Neurobiology and Behavior at Columbia University. Trained in cognitive science and computer science, I study how the brain construct internal models in this ever changing world to guide learning and ecision-making, and what happens when these processes are altered. I use computational models to connect behavior, brains, physiology, and machines.

Aside from science, I love writing, designing with different mediums with an user-centered approach, and exploring the intersection of art and technology. Welcome to my scientific and creative space!
